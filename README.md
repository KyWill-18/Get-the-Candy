# Get the Candy!
A game by Kyla Williams

A kid with a stick who's birthday party was ruined by an evil pinata who stole all his candy. Now he has to explore a 2D world, attacking every evil pinata in sight to make candy fall out of them and collect all his birthday candy back.

### Controls
- A/D or Left/Right Arrow: Move
- W or Up Arrow: Jump
- SPACE: Attack
